01 Transit operations · Toronto

Toronto's subway delays nearly doubled. What's behind them?

Finding: passengers, not trains. Three-quarters of the extra delay comes from disorderly behaviour, people on the tracks, alarms and medical emergencies, while train breakdowns fell 12%. A natural experiment shows Line 1's new signals halved its signal delays.

  • Python
  • Statistics
  • Data cleaning

  • +85%delay minutes per year, 2014–16 to 2023–25
  • 76%of the increase from passengers and the public
  • 267kmessy incident records cleaned, with 18 automated checks

02 Experimentation · Mobile game

Should a mobile game move its first gate from level 30 to 40?

Finding: a health check the usual analysis skips shows the random split was broken: gate 40 got about 800 extra players who barely played. Fixing it cuts the famous effect to less than half and makes it uncertain. Keep the gate at 30, but re-run the test.

  • A/B testing
  • Python
  • SQL
  • Statistics

  • −0.4 ptsreal effect on 7-day retention, against −0.8 in the usual analysis
  • 13unit tests on a reusable statistics toolkit

03 Customer analytics · Telecom

Who will cancel, and who's worth saving?

Finding: judge the model by profit, not accuracy. Targeting customers whose expected profit is positive (risk × value of their bill) beats the usual 50% cut-off in all 30 cost scenarios tested, and offering the deal to everyone loses money.
