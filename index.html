P/01Sports analytics · IPL 2025

Moneyball IPL: which ₹-crore buys were busts, and which were steals?

Price explained only about a tenth of what players actually delivered. Jasprit Bumrah and ₹1-crore Jaydev Unadkat were steals. ₹27-crore Rishabh Pant was among the costliest flops.

  • 10%of on-field impact explained by price
  • 36%of budget buys never played a match
  • 0.73correlation of my metric with team win rates

Built a "runs added" metric from every ball of the season in SQL, matched 226 auction prices to scorecard names, and checked that the metric predicts winning before trusting it.

P/02Environment & health · Delhi

Who's really choking Delhi: Diwali crackers or Punjab's stubble fires?

Both, but not equally. Stubble smoke drives most of early November's smog. Diwali night is one of the year's worst, but it's brief. Over the whole winter, most of the pollution is Delhi's own.

  • ~60%of early-November PM2.5 from stubble smoke
  • ~2%Diwali's pollution compared with the stubble season
  • 84%of winter exposure from Delhi's own pollution

Combined 600,000+ NASA satellite fire detections with five years of air-quality and weather data. A placebo test showed the obvious regression was wrong, so I built a weather-normalisation model instead.

  • Python
  • statsmodels
  • scikit-learn
  • APIs

P/03Finance & economics · India

UPI won. So why is India holding more cash than ever?

Because cash was never just for paying. UPI now moves the equivalent of 91% of GDP a year, yet cash is still 12% of GDP, where it was before UPI existed. UPI replaced cash for spending, not for saving.

  • 91%UPI's yearly value as a share of GDP
  • 12%cash as a share of GDP, unchanged
  • 1.1%average error of my cash-demand forecast

SQL pipeline over RBI and World Bank data with 15 automated data-quality checks, a Holt-Winters forecast of cash demand tested on 25 past periods, and a Power BI dashboard.

  • SQL
  • Power BI
  • DAX
  • Forecasting
