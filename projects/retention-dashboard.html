Risks are out-of-fold predictions from a logistic regression (5-fold cross-validation), so no customer is scored by a model that saw them. "Expected profit" is the model's own estimate. Scored against what customers actually did, the same targeting at the default settings earned about $92,000. Data: IBM's Telco Customer Churn sample (a fictional company).
